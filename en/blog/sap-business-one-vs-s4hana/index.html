SAP Business One or S/4HANA: how to know which one your company needs

Choosing an ERP isn't about picking «the biggest one», but the one that fits how your company operates today and where it wants to go. In practice, the decision between SAP Business One and SAP S/4HANA comes down to three variables: the size and complexity of your operation, transaction volume and growth projection.

SAP Business One

Built for small and mid-sized companies that need to integrate finance, sales, inventory, purchasing and production on a single platform, with a fast implementation and a predictable total cost. It's the core that most retail and hospitality businesses build their operation on.

VS

SAP S/4HANA

Comes into play when the organization reaches a scale and complexity, multiple entities, very high transaction volume, processes that demand real-time analytics, that justify an enterprise platform. It isn't «the better version» of Business One: it's a tool for a different problem.

Our advice is simple: don't over-size. A well-implemented Business One supports years of growth, and migrating to S/4HANA makes sense when the business genuinely calls for it.
