Unified commerce: one core for store, e-commerce and mobile sales

Multichannel isn't the same as unified. Many retailers sell in-store, on e-commerce and on marketplaces, but each channel lives in its own system: stock drifts out of sync, the customer is duplicated and financial reconciliation becomes a monthly headache.

Multichannel

Each channel lives in its own system: stock drifts out of sync, the customer is duplicated and financial reconciliation becomes a monthly headache.

→

Unified commerce

A single core, SAP Business One, beneath every channel. Point of sale, the online store, mobile sales and loyalty all read and write against the same source of truth: one inventory, one customer, one set of books.

01

The payoff, in the operation

  • Reliable stock promises
  • Ship-from-nearest-store
  • Consistent prices and promotions across channels
  • Financial close without parallel spreadsheets

The customer experiences one brand, not five disconnected systems.

You don't have to replace everything at once. The usual path is to consolidate the ERP core and POS first, then add e-commerce, mobile sales and loyalty on top of that base. Every channel you integrate adds value without breaking what already works.
