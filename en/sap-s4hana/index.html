SAP S/4HANA · Intelligent ERP

The intelligent ERP for large-scale operations

SAP S/4HANA is SAP's next-generation ERP suite, built on the in-memory HANA database to process, analyze and decide in real time across your company's most critical processes.

Evaluate an S/4HANA project Compare with Business One

The technology foundation for the intelligent enterprise

Unlike a traditional ERP, S/4HANA unifies transactions and analytics on a single in-memory data source. The result: faster closes, continuous planning and an operation that reacts in the moment, not at month's end.

SAP HANA in-memory database: queries and reports in seconds.

Simplified data model on the Universal Journal in finance.

Embedded analytics in every process, no parallel systems.

Modern, role-based user experience with SAP Fiori.

Capabilities

Mission-critical processes, end to end

Real-time finance

Accounting, treasury and control with accelerated close on the Universal Journal and embedded analytics.

Supply chain

Planning, inventory and logistics with end-to-end visibility and real-time response.

Advanced manufacturing

Production planning, live MRP and plant management for industrial operations.

Procurement & sourcing

Sourcing, contracts and supplier management integrated with the financial flow.

Sales & service

Full commercial cycle, pricing and after-sales connected to inventory and finance.

Embedded AI & analytics

Predictive scenarios, assistants and intelligent automation inside every process.

Built for the complexity of the large enterprise

S/4HANA is the right choice when volume, regulatory complexity or international scale clearly outgrow what a standard ERP can sustain.

High transaction volumes and multiple companies or countries.

Advanced manufacturing or logistics processes that demand real time.

Continuous analytics and planning at a corporate level.

Data and AI strategy as a competitive advantage.
