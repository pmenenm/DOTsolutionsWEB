The diagnosis

From manual invoicing to automatic compliance

Does your invoicing fail to scale with your growing operation?

The platform automatically adapts to your document volume and company size.

Does an internet outage stop your invoicing?

Keeps working even without a connection: generates and validates documents, and syncs as soon as the connection is back.

Worried about documents rejected by the SII?

Automatic checks on every DTE before issuing, avoiding errors and invalid documents.

Need backup if the SII requests old documents?

How it works

How each DTE is managed

Our DTE platform issues tax documents under current SII regulations, with automatic validations and digital distribution to customers and the SII, with native integration to SAP Business One.

  1. 01

    Electronic signature

    Request an Advanced Electronic Signature (FIEL) from the SII.

  2. 02

    Authorization

    Invoice authorization via a security device and barcode.

  3. 03

    Issuing and distribution

    Printing and distributing the DTE to your customer and the SII.

  4. 04

    Tracking

    Full tracking from issuing through to commercial acceptance.
