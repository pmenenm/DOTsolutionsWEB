The pieces

Eight world-class pieces, one single flow

Your financial health, under control

SAP Business One — Live per-location balance sheet and P&L, multi-tax-ID accounting, cash flow and bank/payment reconciliation, no parallel spreadsheets.

Tables, kitchen and payments, friction-free

TCPOS — Sales, tables, orders, KDS, self-service kiosk and payment methods. Works offline with no in-store server: if the internet goes down, your register keeps charging.

Purchasing that anticipates. Automatically.

Smart Order — Cross-references SAP's historical purchases with TCPOS's real sales to suggest what and how much to order. Live production, recipes and costs: real margin, not estimated.

Your business live, anywhere

BiPyxis — TCPOS sales, Smart Order purchasing and SAP accounting on one reconciled dashboard. F&B KPIs ready to go: food cost, average ticket, dish mix.

No lines. No waiting. No annoyed customers.

AyHungry — Digitizes service and reservations: advance orders, table management, service from the customer's phone, and sentiment analysis for every visit.

Build loyalty, sell and deliver. All your brand.

Loyalty — Loyalty natively integrated into TCPOS, your own e-commerce channel and last-mile delivery connected to aggregators, with no middleman commission.

Delivery, straight to the kitchen

ChefCloud — Rappi, PedidosYa, Uber Eats and more all land on the same TCPOS and the same order ticket. Zero extra tablets, auto-acceptance and automatic kitchen printing.

Receipts and invoices that never fail

DTE Invoicing — Issues all your tax documents online, natively integrated with SAP and TCPOS. Keeps invoicing even without internet, and automatically complies with the SII.
