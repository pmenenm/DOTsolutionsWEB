Retail management, simple and omnichannel

RetailPro Prism is the retail management system that centralizes sales, inventory and analytics for physical stores, kiosks and e-commerce on one platform.

With native integration to SAP Business One, RetailPro Prism connects POS, back office and inventory in real time. It scales without friction, from a single store to a franchise network, without losing control or visibility.

Talk to an expert

The diagnosis

From fragmented management to omnichannel control

Are you managing stores, kiosks and e-commerce as separate systems?

RetailPro Prism centralizes POS, back office and inventory in a single platform, so your physical and digital operation works as one.

Does your inventory fail to reflect what's happening in real time?

Sales and stock update instantly across every channel, so you never sell something you no longer have.

Does every new store or franchise mean reconfiguring everything from scratch?

Scale across formats: stores, franchises and digital channels, with centralized support and a consistent experience across devices.

Are you deciding with outdated reports instead of live data?

Custom analytics and reports show you which product, store or channel is truly profitable, today.

How it works

An integration process built for your business

RetailPro Prism is the modular extension of Retail Pro (A Nayax Company) that adapts your operational flow with remote updates and a consistent experience on any device: Windows, iOS, Android, POS or desktop.

  1. 01

    Diagnosis

    We map your current sales, inventory and service processes across every channel.

  2. 02

    Integration architecture

    We design how RetailPro Prism connects to SAP Business One and the rest of your stack.

  3. 03

    Setup and go-live

    We customize modules, branding and sales flow, train your team, and go live.
