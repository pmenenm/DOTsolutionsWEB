Vertical solutions

Designed for your industry

Cover the specific needs of industries like transportation, maintenance, manufacturing, agribusiness, retail and distribution.

Credit Card Operator Reconciliation

Automates reconciliation of Transbank or Getnet deposits against your card sales.

+

The Credit Card reconciliation add-on is essential for companies operating with Transbank or Getnet. It automates the reconciliation of deposits received against card payments from sales recorded in SAP Business One. The solution imports settlement files, performing an intelligent match between the amounts deposited by the operator and the sales payments received (credit/debit). This lets you automatically post the fees and withholdings applied, ensuring the accounting balance matches the real balance. It eliminates manual reconciliation work, guarantees accuracy and significantly speeds up the financial close.

TransbankGetnet

Warehouse Management (WMS) — Produmex

Storage rules, efficient picking and mobile scanning for your warehouses.

+

Produmex WMS and Produmex Scan offer an advanced solution to optimize warehouse management in SAP Business One. Produmex WMS provides the business intelligence to implement complex storage rules, optimize the use of locations and define high-efficiency picking strategies. Produmex Scan is the mobile component that lets warehouse operators run every product-handling task, from receiving and cycle counting to final dispatch, using mobile scanning devices. This eliminates paper, drastically reduces inventory errors and ensures precise traceability of every item.

Apparel & Footwear

Size and color product matrix for fashion, footwear and apparel.

+

The Apparel & Footwear add-on complements SAP Business One to meet the complex needs of fashion, footwear and apparel companies managing multiple product variants. Its main function is to replace individual item management with an intuitive Product Matrix (styles), allowing quick product creation based on attributes like color and size, greatly simplifying data entry. It makes inventory management easier by letting you view and control stock for every variant combination on a single screen. It also optimizes purchasing, sales and production processes with specialized tools, such as collection planning, season-based procurement and size/color order management.

Mobile One

Turns every salesperson into a mobile office: catalog, pricing, stock and field sales.

+

Mobile One is an app designed to extend SAP Business One's functionality to your field sales team. Its goal is to turn every salesperson into a mobile office, eliminating paperwork and double data entry. The add-on provides real-time access to critical information from a sales terminal: product catalog, customer-specific price lists and up-to-date inventory levels. Salespeople can create sales receipts or invoices directly at the point of sale, with automatic stock validation, register payment and generate the electronic sales document. All sales data is integrated into SAP Business One instantly and accurately.

Enterpryze

A mobile, cloud suite that brings SAP Business One to every employee, in and out of the office.

+

Enterpryze is a suite of modular, mobile, cloud-based applications designed to extend SAP Business One's functionality to every employee at your company, especially those working outside the office. It acts as a friendly bridge between SAP Business One's complexity and end users, with an intuitive interface for specific tasks. Its goal is to improve productivity in the field and ensure that all operations and data are captured and synced in real time.

  • Mobile sales & CRM — Customer data, live inventory, orders and quotes from any device.
  • Field service management — Work orders, technician assignment, time tracking and customer signature.
  • Expense & finance management — Capture and approval of travel expenses from the phone's camera.
  • Operations & inventory — Mobile cycle-counting and transfer functions.

Farm4all — Agricultural Management

Full traceability and precise costs by product, batch, field block or season.

+

Farm4all is a specialized add-on that integrates the operational and financial management of the agricultural and agribusiness sector directly with SAP Business One. It incorporates the complex logic of crop cycles and field production. Its goal is to ensure full production traceability and precise unit-cost calculation by product, batch, field block or season, with constant monitoring and real-time budget control.

  • Campaigns & field blocks — Planning and budget control by agricultural campaign and land unit.
  • Agricultural work orders — Sowing, irrigation, fertilizing and pruning, with labor, machinery and input costs.
  • Inventory traceability — From inputs (seeds, agrochemicals) to harvest records.
  • Financial integration — Field costs are automatically posted to SAP Business One's accounting.

Our AddOns

Horizontal, for any industry

Solutions that enhance the standard modules (Finance, Inventory, Sales) and apply to virtually any industry.

Electronic Invoicing — Issuance

Issue, stamp and send every tax document in compliance with SII regulations.

+

The Electronic Invoicing solution for SAP Business One manages the full tax-document cycle in compliance with Chilean regulations (SII). It automates the issuance, stamping and sending of every Electronic Tax Document (DTE) — invoices, credit notes, debit notes, receipts and dispatch guides — directly from sales documents in SAP Business One.

Electronic Invoicing — Automatic Receipt

Receives and validates your suppliers' invoices and creates purchase drafts on its own.

+

Streamlines the purchasing cycle by automatically integrating supplier invoices. It receives the XML files, automatically validates them with the SII and efficiently creates purchase document drafts in SAP Business One. This eliminates manual data entry, reduces operational errors and ensures tax traceability and real-time financial control.

Fixed Asset Tax Adjustment

Automatic monetary correction of fixed assets per SII (CPI) regulations.

+

The Monetary Correction add-on for SAP Business One's Fixed Asset module is essential for accounting compliance under Chilean (SII) regulations. It automatically calculates and adjusts asset values and accumulated depreciation based on official price indexes (like the CPI), generating correction journal entries in bulk and on a periodic basis. It ensures balance sheets reflect the real economic value of assets.

Bank Statement Import

Imports bank statements and speeds up reconciliation with intelligent matching.

+

Eliminates manual entry of bank account movements in SAP Business One. It enables direct, automated import of bank transactions from multiple formats (CSV, TXT) into the treasury module, and performs intelligent matching of bank movements against recorded payments and deposits. It reduces errors and ensures balances reflect the real daily cash position.

Payment File Generation

Generates the consolidated payment file in the format your bank requires.

+

Automates the treasury process in SAP Business One by generating a consolidated payment file (suppliers, payroll or services) in the structured, secure format your bank requires. It takes transactions from the treasury module and generates a flat file ready for direct upload to the bank portal. It eliminates manual entry for each payment and speeds up the treasury cycle.

Daily Exchange Rate Update

Automatically updates official exchange rates from the Central Bank.

+

A service for managing SAP Business One's daily exchange rates across currencies. It automates the reliable daily load of official values directly from the Central Bank. It connects, downloads the day's rate and updates the Exchange Rate table. It eliminates manual entry errors and ensures every foreign-currency transaction and report reflects accurate values.

Intercompany

Automatically replicates master data and transactions between your subsidiaries or companies.

+

An ideal solution for companies with multiple subsidiaries or companies running separate SAP Business One databases. It automates the replication of master data and transactions between them: when Company A issues a sales invoice, it automatically creates the vendor invoice in Company B. It eliminates double entry, speeds up internal settlement and makes financial consolidation and cross-entity inventory management easier.
